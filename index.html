DocYonder
Business plan · November 2026
Prepared by Aprampal · docyonder.com

Find the right doctor, near you or around the world.

DocYonder explains a person's symptoms or medical report in plain language, then shows two paths: well-reviewed doctors nearby, and, for treatments people commonly travel for, trusted destinations abroad with typical prices. It earns money by connecting patients who choose treatment abroad with partner clinics and travel services.

Product is live at www.docyonder.com

  • Lab results are hard to read. Patients often get reports days before an appointment and don't know which values matter.
  • People don't know which doctor to see. Many start with a general search and end up at the wrong specialist.
  • Waits and prices push people abroad, with little guidance. Dental work, cosmetic surgery, IVF and some operations are often slow or expensive at home, and most information about going abroad comes from sites that sell the trip.

What DocYonder does today

  • Live

    Symptom check. A person describes how they feel; AI gives a preliminary read, picks the right kind of doctor, and flags possible emergencies.

  • Live

    Report explainer. Upload a lab result, scan report or photo (including scanned PDFs). Each result is listed with its normal range and marked low, high or normal, then explained in plain language.

  • Live

    Doctors near you, anywhere. Uses the visitor's location or a typed city to show nearby doctors from Google Maps, with distance, rating, opening hours and Call, Directions and Message.

  • Live

    Treatment abroad. For nine treatments people commonly travel for, shows well-known destinations, typical prices against home, what packages include, and top-rated clinics, with a "Get a free quote" button.

  • Live

    Accounts and privacy. Saved reports, password reset by email, owner-only access to medical files, and one free try of each feature for guests.

How a patient moves through DocYonder

  1. Starts with their own situation. Describes symptoms or uploads a report.

  2. Understands it. Gets a plain-language explanation and the right kind of specialist.

  3. Sees local care first. Nearby doctors they can call today.

  4. Compares going abroad, when it makes sense. Destinations, typical prices and well-reviewed clinics.

  5. Requests a quote. DocYonder's team matches them with partner clinics and helps plan travel.

Who it's for and how we reach them

  • Canadians facing long waits or uninsured costs, especially for dental implants, IVF, cosmetic and orthopaedic care.
  • US patients paying out of pocket, where the price gap is largest.
  • Diaspora communities who already travel home and want trusted clinics there.
  • Channels: search (people already type "dental implants abroad"), short explainer videos on reading lab results, community groups, and referral partnerships with clinics.

Competition

Medical travel platforms such as Bookimed, PlacidWay and MyMediTravel already list clinics and prices. They start from the sale: a procedure and a destination.

DocYonder starts from the patient's own symptoms or report, shows local care first, and only suggests going abroad when it fits. Prices come with sources, and ratings come from Google Maps rather than the clinics themselves. That neutrality is the trust advantage, and it has to be protected as partner clinics join.

Risks and how we handle them

Medical advice and liability

Every result is labelled a preliminary explanation, urgent findings trigger emergency guidance, and patients are told to see a doctor. Legal review and liability insurance before taking payments.

Health data privacy

Files are private to their owner and secrets stay off the code. Next: privacy policy, consent screens and a PIPEDA/PHIPA review.

Selling travel

Start with affiliate links, where the travel company makes the sale. Selling travel directly from Ontario would likely require TICO registration.

Conflicts of interest

Commissions could bias recommendations. Partner status will always be disclosed, and sponsored placement kept separate from ratings.

AI accuracy

The report explainer reads the document directly and is checked for consistency; results are structured so errors are visible. Ongoing testing with real reports is planned.
